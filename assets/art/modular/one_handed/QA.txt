ONE-HANDED MODULAR ART - FINAL BATTLE-SCALE ASSETS

Final sheets (RGBA, 2048x1792, eight columns, seven rows):
body.png - shared unarmed character, cream tunic, trousers, boots.
armor.png - independent bandit vest/scarf, no complete person.
weapon.png - wooden clubs only, no skin.
sword.png - steel swords only, no skin.
warband_armor.png - independent blue/steel outer armor, no complete person.
foreground.png - exact canonical body RGB with authored head/arm alpha masks.

Final rigs:
game/content/modular/one_handed/club.tres (Club bandit)
game/content/modular/one_handed/warband.tres (Sword warband)
Both share body.tres and foreground.tres. head_foreground.tres is an unused
intermediate resource, not part of either final rig.
Armor registration is baked into its independent atlas. Weapon regions,
pivots, scales and rotations register the separate equipment to 56 hand poses.

Generation and finishing:
Initial artwork was generated with built-in image_gen; submitted prompts are
in GENERATION_PROMPTS.txt. Original generated normalized sheets are preserved
in source_generated/. After explicit user authorization to finish with
pixel/mask editing, finish_masks.py used Pillow/NumPy to bake calibrated armor
registration, constrain its alpha to the body silhouette, cut head/arm holes,
and build foreground from exact accepted body pixels. It changes neither
body identity nor original source atlases. No armor or weapon is baked into
body.png. Do not run calibrate.py alone against final finished sheets;
finish_masks.py restores its source inputs before calibration and finishing.

Validation:
All six final sheets have actual alpha transparency and identical dimensions.
Automated comparison confirms every visible foreground RGB pixel equals the
canonical body RGB at exactly the same coordinate.
Godot rig.validate() returns [] for both rigs; all eight referenced layer
instances report 56 frames and 2048x1792 sheets. Validator exits zero, with
Godot shutdown resource/ObjectDB retention warnings from this small harness.
All 56 club and 56 warband composites were inspected in Godot galleries.
The parent reviewed the final sleeve/vest improvement as sufficient for the
battle-size target. Final PNGs are frozen for parent import and gameplay QA.

Art limitations:
Some back-facing shoulder and armhole contours remain stylized and visible
at enlarged inspection scale, especially warband poses 12-15 and 36-43.
Grip registration is visually calibrated, not an assertion of exact anatomical
pixel correspondence. These are battle-scale assets, not close-up portraits.

Generation history:
