Hero modular artwork review

Four independent generated layers, all normalized to 2048x1792:
body.png: plain blue under-tunic, dark trousers, boots, bare hands; no armor or weapon.
armor.png: detached brown torso armor only; no full characters.
weapon.png: greatsword only; no skin. Drawn behind body for hand occlusion.
foreground.png: exact accepted-body pixels under fitted head/arm masks. Retained as an optional authoring/debug layer; not equipped by default.

All 56 poses inspected in all_frames_preview.png. Enlarged movement/attack/death samples in calibrated_preview.png.
Death body clipping was repaired through a second body image generation edit.
Weapon regions use measured connected-component bounds rather than fixed cells: horizontal strike tips and falling blades extend across original cell boundaries.
Armor placements use blue torso area measurements followed by visual review. Final registration is baked into the standard 8x7 atlas.
After user authorization for deterministic pixel editing, armor alpha was clipped to the exact body silhouette and cut by fitted head/arm masks. Body pixels now show directly through the equipment. No independently repainted head or arm pixels are displayed.
All 56 armor frames retain nonempty independent clothing; no armor alpha exists outside body alpha.
Weapon artwork and connected-component overscan registrations were preserved unchanged by this finishing pass.

The active rig uses three layers: body, armor, weapon. Foreground is unnecessary because the armor contains occlusion cutouts.
Reproducible finishing scripts: refine_foreground.py then refine_armor.py. Their saved source sheets and registrations preserve the generated inputs.
Exact imagegen prompts, including rejected iterations, are in GENERATION_PROMPTS.txt.
Artwork was generated using the built-in imagegen tool. Godot resized/package-rendered the PNGs. Initially Python only measured registration; after explicit user authorization, Pillow/numpy performed deterministic alpha masking and exact-body pixel extraction.
